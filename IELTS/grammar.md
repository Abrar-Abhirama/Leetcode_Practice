1. The rules for articles in IELTS writing (a, an , the)

- General plural: no article
- general uncountable: no article
- Specific: use the
- First mention a, later mentions the
- A before consonant sounds, an before vowel sound
- Superlatives and unique things take the


2. Countable and Uncountable nouns

- much & many -> more
- few -> fewer & little -> less
- number & amount
- Each & every

3. Adjective order in English

- OSASCOMP rule
- Opinion, size, age, shape, colour, origin, material, purpose.
